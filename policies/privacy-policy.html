Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026 | App: Cliq Photoshoot Pose

This Privacy Policy explains how Cygnux ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application Cliq Photoshoot Pose (the "Application" / "App").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, browsing photoshoot poses, or contacting us:

  • Account Information: If you register or log in, we may collect your username, email address, profile name, and password credentials (encrypted).
  • User-Generated Content: Photos and custom poses that you choose to upload, favorite, or crop within the application.
  • Device Information & Identifiers: Advertising ID (Google AAID), device model, OS version, IP address, and general network diagnostics to help troubleshoot crashes.

2. Permissions and How We Use Them

Photo, Media & Camera Access

  • Photos & Videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / Storage): The App requests access to your photos and videos exclusively to allow you to select images from your gallery to crop, align with photoshoot pose guides, and preview your poses. We do not access, scan, or share any unrelated private media.
  • Camera Permission: Required only when you use the in-app camera viewfinder to align live photography poses with real-time silhouette templates.
  • Set Wallpaper: Used when you explicitly choose to set a pose or animated live wallpaper onto your device background.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to send updates about trending photography poses and app features. You can disable this anytime in device settings.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The Application integrates with trusted third-party providers for advertising, authentication, push notifications, and analytics:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Facebook Audience Network
  • Wortise SDK
  • AppLovin
  • StartApp (Start.io)
  • OneSignal Push Notifications

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide app services. You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, visit our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Page or email us at support@cygnux.in with your registered username/email.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Application is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we take immediate steps to delete such data from our servers.

6. Security of Your Information

We use industry-standard administrative and technical security measures (including HTTPS encryption) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
