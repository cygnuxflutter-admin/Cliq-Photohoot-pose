Account & Data Deletion Request

For App: Cliq Photoshoot Pose | Developed by Cygnux

At Cliq Photoshoot Pose, we respect your privacy and provide you with complete control over your personal account and associated data. In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, you can request full deletion of your account and all related data using the methods below.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

  • Data that will be permanently deleted:
    • Your registered username, full name, and email address.
    • Your profile details, avatar, and authentication credentials.
    • Your saved favorite poses, bookmarks, and custom preferences.
    • Any user-uploaded content or feedback submitted under your account.
  • Data retention policy:
    • All personal data is permanently deleted from our primary servers within 7 to 30 days of request verification.
    • We do not retain unnecessary historical user data once an account is deleted. Non-identifiable aggregated analytics (e.g. total app downloads) may be retained.

How to Request Account Deletion

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the Cliq Photoshoot Pose app on your device.
  2. Go to Profile / Settings tab.
  3. Select "Delete Account" and confirm your choice.
